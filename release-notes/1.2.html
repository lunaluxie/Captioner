Speakers and watermarks

  • Speakers: turn on Speakers in the inspector (or the Captions sheet on iPhone and iPad) and Captioner listens for who is talking and colors each caption by voice: yellow, cyan, green and magenta for up to four speakers. Let it count the voices itself or tell it how many there are; any caption's speaker can be changed by hand, and the four colors are part of the style. The first use downloads a 22 MB speaker model; after that it works offline.
  • Watermark: add a logo or handle over the video with its own opacity, size, margin and a nine-spot position grid. New videos start with the last watermark you used.
