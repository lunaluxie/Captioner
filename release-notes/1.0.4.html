Translate your captions

  • A new “Translate to” menu in the inspector turns the captions into any of the languages Apple's on-device translation supports: Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi and more. Whole sentences are translated, then laid back over the original speech so the captions keep their timing. Pick the original language to go back.
  • Chinese, Japanese and Thai captions are split into words for the word-by-word highlight.
