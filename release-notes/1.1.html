Caption files and wide screens

  • Save the captions as a file: File › Save Captions As, or the Caption file menu in the inspector, writes SubRip (.srt), WebVTT (.vtt) or plain text (.txt), with the transcription controls applied.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the Captions sheet saves the same files into Captioner's folder in Files and opens the share sheet, and the Share button after an export now opens the share sheet for the video.
  • On iPad and an unfolded iPhone, the editor shows the video on the left and the captions beside it.
